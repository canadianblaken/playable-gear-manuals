Hapax interactive manual

Unofficial, playable recreation of the Squarp Hapax sequencer, built from the Hapax manual (hapaxOS 3.10, June 2026), the online effects chapter, the 20 Squarp tutorial videos and the 3.20–3.22 firmware notes. Hapax makes no sound; here every MIDI channel has a stand-in instrument in your browser. Not affiliated with Squarp instruments.

The manual, chapter by chapter

Show jumps the simulation to that part of the Hapax and explains it here. Chapters with a tutorial video link to it.

    About this simulation

    Two projects with 16 tracks × 16 patterns, POLY / DRUM / MPE / AFTR tracks, live scale/chord/drum modes, the step sequencer (zoom, pages, selections, warp, loop points, math conditions, roll, chance, uTime, mono edit, note learn, step-rec), automation lanes, pattern mode with sync, follow actions, sections and songs, mixmute, the transpose track (TRSP and match chord), pScale, time signatures, elasticity, the modMatrix and most effects, algorithms, snapshot, undo, recording (countdown, punch-in, looper, overdub/hard rec, multitrack), MIDI file export/import and Web MIDI in/out all work.

    Simplified or not simulated
    • Sounds are stand-ins; CV/Gate outputs play a mono synth; CV inputs, DIN sync, clock sync and pedal are settings only.
    • ARPOLY, ENVELOPE and SPRING show their parameters but don't process notes; NRPN, CC pairs and program change lanes aren't sent.
    • MPE tracks play like POLY tracks (no per-note expression curves); AFTR notes carry one pressure value rather than a drawn curve.
    • The chord-mode pad layout follows Squarp's diagram but the exact position of each modifier is approximate. Live drum 4- and 1-velocity layouts are approximations.
    • Instrument definitions: a few built-in examples instead of files on an SD card. Projects you save stay in this browser.
    • Hardware multi-touch is replaced by holding with right-click (or long-press on touch) and then pressing other controls.

    Sources: Hapax manual (updated June 22 2026, hapaxOS 3.10), squarp.net/hapax/manual (effects, project chapters), HapaxOS release notes 3.20–3.22, Hapax quickstart, “HAPAX TUTORIALS” playlist. Page numbers refer to the PDF manual.
