SQUID interactive manual

Unofficial fan recreation of the Toraiz SQUID multitrack sequencer (firmware 1.13). Front-panel layout and behaviour from the Operating Instructions + v1.10 addendum; screen text taken from the v1.13 firmware. Not affiliated with Pioneer DJ / AlphaTheta.

Button combinations

Right-click or long-press (latch) any control marked +, or hold a pad, and the controls it combines with light up blue; hover one to read the combo. Hover a row below to see both controls, click Show to watch it.

About this simulation

The screen, menus, pad modes, step editing, pattern sets, mutes, copy/paste, interpolation, randomizer, running direction, speed and rhythmic controls, trigger probability and speed modulation all work. Turn on Sound to hear it through a built-in drum kit and synths, or pick one of the Example projects.

Simplified or not simulated
  • No MIDI/CV/sync output; the rear jacks are labels only.
  • Sounds are a built-in drum kit and simple synths standing in for whatever the real tracks would drive over MIDI/CV. Drum tracks use the General MIDI drum map (36 kick, 38 snare, 42 closed hat…).
  • Swing delays the audio of even steps; the pads still light on the grid. Groove Bend shifts the current track only.
  • Arpeggiator, Note Repeat and real-time automation recording are display-only.
  • Chord-mode voicings and default Harmonizer chords are illustrative; the 18 chord set names come from the firmware.
  • Time Warp shows the mode and pad layout but doesn't record.
  • Skip-back direction is approximated (two steps forward, one back).
  • Projects: Create New and Restore Demo work; other file operations only show their screens.
