KeyStep Pro interactive manual

Unofficial and playable, firmware 2.5. Not affiliated with Arturia.A playable recreation of the Arturia KeyStep Pro, built from the 2.5.1 user manual, the MIDI Control Center device definition and a reverse-engineering study of firmware 2.5.20. All sounds are synthesized stand-ins. The Device tools tab talks to a real KeyStep Pro over Web MIDI, or to this simulation.

A real KeyStep needs Chrome or Edge on a computer, with SysEx permission. Safari, including on iPad, has no Web MIDI; the manual still works there.

Show jumps the simulation to that part of the KeyStep Pro and explains it here.

    About this simulation

    Four tracks with 16 patterns of up to 64 steps (up to 16 notes per step), the drum sequencer with 24 lanes, three arpeggiators, scales and user scales, chord mode, transpose (with groups and latch), quick edit, step edit, step and real-time recording, overdub, the Shift step functions, step skip, pages, chains, scenes, projects with save/copy/erase/recall, the looper strip, control mode, the Utility menu, tempo, swing, metronome and Web MIDI in/out all work.

    Firmware notes

    Some behaviour here follows the decoded 2.5.20 firmware rather than the manual. Those places are marked with a blue “Firmware” note in the help text.

    Simplified or not simulated
    • Sounds are stand-ins; CV/Gate outputs, clock in/out, sync and pedal are settings only.
    • Arpeggiator randomness, keyboard splits and the Program Change assign page are simplified.
    • Copy/paste of individual notes inside a step and the Chroma colour sets are not simulated.
    • Multi-touch is replaced by holding with right-click (or long-press on touch) and then pressing other controls.

    Sources: KeyStep Pro user manual 2.5.1 (14 March 2024), MIDI Control Center device file, firmware 2.5.20 reverse-engineering notes. Section numbers refer to the manual.
