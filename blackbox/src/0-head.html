Blackbox interactive manual

Unofficial, playable recreation of the 1010music Blackbox (firmware 3.0), built from the Blackbox 3.0 User Manual, with an optional layer for the community blackbox-mod 3.1.n. All sounds are synthesized in your browser; no 1010music samples, firmware or artwork are used. Not affiliated with 1010music.

Tap = press · long-press = hold · drag a knob up/down = turn · tap anything for its explanationHover anything for help · right-click = hold a button · wheel or drag a knob to turn · 1–8 nav buttons · Space PLAY · . STOP · R REC · I INFO · Esc BACK

The manual, chapter by chapter

Show jumps the simulation to that part of the Blackbox and explains it here.

    About this simulation

    Pads (Sample, Multi-sample, Clip, Slicer, Granular), ADSR, filter, LFO and modulation, the 16×4 sequencer with piano roll, probability and velocity, Song scenes with Keep and Grab, both effects, the 4-band EQ, mixer and mutes, presets and the Tools pages all work. You can record from your microphone or resample the output, and import your own samples.

    Simplified or not simulated
    • One stereo output; Out 1/2/3 are mixed together. The rear jacks, CV and analog clock are labels only.
    • Sounds are synthesized stand-ins for the factory library; folder names are invented.
    • Slicer Sync, MID file import, New Multi (automated multi-sample recording), Pack, Clean and SD Mode only show their screens or a message.
    • Presets you save live in this browser (localStorage); recorded and imported audio lasts for the session only.
    • Pinch-zoom on the waveform and piano roll is the mouse wheel here.
    • Mod layer: Munchi granular delay falls back to the stock delay; Fidelity machine modes are approximations.
